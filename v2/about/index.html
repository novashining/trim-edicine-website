About TRIM-edicine

From discovery to therapeutic opportunity

TRIM-edicine is a privately held biotechnology company focused on translating distinctive protein and peptide biology into approaches for tissue repair, regenerative medicine, age-related dysfunction and oncology.

Biopharmaceutical developmentTranslational researchPartnership driven
Our company

Built around biology with broad therapeutic relevance

TRIM-edicine's historical portfolio spans membrane repair, tissue protection, cellular homeostasis, aging biology and peptide therapeutics for cancer.

TRIM-edicine is engaged in the development of novel biopharmaceutical products addressing important unmet medical needs.

The company's historical work has included applications in tissue repair, ischemia/reperfusion injury, dermal wounds, oral health, sports medicine, muscular dystrophy, age-related dysfunction and oncology.

Research and development has emphasized proof-of-concept studies, translational validation, intellectual property development and partnerships that can move promising discoveries toward therapeutic applications.

How we work

A translational path from mechanism to development

The legacy company model combines scientific discovery with development partnerships rather than treating basic biology and commercialization as separate activities.

01 / DISCOVERY

Identify distinctive biology

Focus on proteins and peptides with strong mechanistic relevance to cell injury, repair, homeostasis and disease.

02 / TRANSLATION

Build proof of concept

Develop therapeutic approaches, validation studies and preclinical evidence around the most promising biological mechanisms.

03 / PARTNERSHIP

Advance with collaborators

Engage academic, government and industry collaborators, investors, development partners and potential licensees.

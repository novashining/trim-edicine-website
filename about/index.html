About TRIM-edicine

Translating distinctive biology into therapeutic opportunity.

TRIM-edicine is a privately held biotechnology company focused on novel biopharmaceutical approaches for unmet medical needs.

Company focus

The company was built around proteins with unusual biological properties that influence cellular pathways altered in disease. Historical R&D efforts have emphasized proof-of-concept research, translational development, and partnerships that can advance these technologies toward patients.

The legacy site identified applications spanning tissue repair, muscular dystrophy, ischemia/reperfusion injury, dermal wounds, oral health, age-related dysfunction, and oncology.

Science-led

Mechanism-driven programs grounded in cell biology and protein therapeutics.

Translational

Historical programs progressed through SBIR support, preclinical development, industry collaboration, and clinical-entry milestones.

Partnership-oriented

The company has historically sought investors, development partners, and licensees to advance its platforms.

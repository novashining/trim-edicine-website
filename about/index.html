About TRIM-edicine

Repairing Life through innovative biopharmaceutical development.

Our Company

TRIM-edicine is a privately held biotechnology company engaged in the development of novel biopharmaceutical products to address important unmet medical needs.

The company has historically focused on protein and peptide technologies with applications spanning tissue repair, ischemia/reperfusion injury, dermal wounds, oral health, sports medicine, muscular dystrophy, age-related dysfunction and oncology.

Research and development has emphasized proof-of-concept studies, translational validation, intellectual property development and partnerships that can move promising discoveries toward therapeutic applications.

Our Approach

Discovery

Identify proteins and peptides with distinctive biological functions relevant to disease and tissue repair.

Translation

Develop proof-of-concept evidence, scalable therapeutic approaches and pathways toward clinical development.

Partnership

Work with academic, government and industry collaborators, investors, development partners and licensees.
